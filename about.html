The designer

Maya Greco.

Trained at Central Saint Martins and seasoned in the cutting rooms of Lemaire and The Row, Maya founded MG Fashion in 2018 with one rule: nothing leaves the studio that she would not wear for ten years.

Each capsule begins with a single fiber — a Belgian linen, a Sardinian wool, a Japanese cupro — and grows outward, slowly, until forty silhouettes find their form.

The atelier is rooted in Gujarat, India, where our artisans and tailors craft and finish every garment by hand.

Process

From fiber to figure

  1. 01

    Sourcing

    Mills are visited in person; each fiber is named on the label.

  2. 02

    Pattern

    Drafted on the half-scale, fitted on a living body, never digital first.

  3. 03

    Cutting

    Single-layer cuts on the table, no industrial spreaders.

  4. 04

    Finishing

    Hand-rolled hems, French seams, signed and numbered.
